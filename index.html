MANIFESTO / FOUNDER

ABOUT

There are moments when we quietly wave to what has ended — not out of longing, nor to pretend we’ve forgotten, but simply to acknowledge that it lived.

This is the stance behind Say Hello To Never Recordings. We move forward while carrying the past, but we do not remain in the same place.

It’s okay to imagine the future without turning it into a promise. Somewhere between the two, the present takes shape.

The work of the label is simple: we deliver the sound and the moments that happen here to the people who might need them. Connecting bands and listeners, allowing a singular moment to reach another place.

Records are not only for preservation; they are a way for something to travel. Shows are not for consumption; they are for sharing what happens when sound meets a room.

No grand narrative is required. We simply carry what is here forward, and let it find who it’s meant for.

Daisuke Fujimoto

Founder, organizer, drummer, tour coordinator. Based in Tokyo, Japan.
Former member of Balloons (1996–2016), Tokyo.
