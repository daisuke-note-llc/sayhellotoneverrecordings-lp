PLAY DEAD SEASON JAPAN-US TOUR 2024

PLAY DEAD SEASON初のUS TOURが決まりました。そして渡米前には高松で、帰国後はSHELTERでワンマン公演が決まっています。各公演予約可能ですので是非チェックしてください。

PLAY DEAD SEASON's first US TOUR has been decided. Before heading to the US, they will have a solo concert in Takamatsu, and after returning, a one-man show at SHELTER is confirmed. Reservations for each show are available, so please be sure to check them out.
